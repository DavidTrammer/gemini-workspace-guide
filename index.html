Google Sheets

Help me organize & Formulas
Core

Generate structured tables, complex nested formulas (QUERY, XLOOKUP), and categorize qualitative data effortlessly.

Key Capabilities
  • "Help me organize": Generates custom tables with sample data.
  • Formula Generator: Translates natural language into multi-condition formulas.
  • Apps Script Automation: Write Google Apps Scripts directly in the side panel.
Featured Prompt:
Create a table to track customer renewals for Q4. Include Account Name, ARR, Contract End Date, Health Score (Green/Yellow/Red), and a calculated column for Renewal Likelihood %.

Gmail

Drafting & Thread Summaries
Core

Cut through inbox clutter. Summarize 30-reply threads in seconds and craft context-aware drafts using "Help me write".

Key Capabilities
  • Thread Summarization: Instant bullet points with action items on web & mobile.
  • Tone Polish: Shift instantly between Formalize, Elaborate, and Shorten.
  • Side Panel Cross-Check: Reference emails against files in Drive simultaneously.
Featured Prompt:
Draft a warm but firm email to follow up on outstanding invoice #INV-4029. Emphasize that services remain active, specify bank wire details, and ask for payment confirmation by Friday.

NotebookLM

Source-Grounded AI Research
Advanced

Google's personalized AI collaborator. Upload PDFs, Docs, web links, and YouTube videos for 100% source-grounded answers.

Key Capabilities
  • Audio Overviews: Converts complex source material into a lively 2-host podcast.
  • Direct Citations: Every single answer links directly to exact source passages.
  • Auto Guides: 1-click generation of Study Guides, Briefing Docs, and FAQs.
Featured Prompt:
Based strictly on the uploaded market research reports, summarize how competitors are pricing enterprise tiers. Provide citations for each competitor and highlight any recurring discounts.

Google Docs

Content Creation & Smart Canvas
Core

Overcome the blank page. Draft structured whitepapers, press releases, client proposals, and summaries with inline AI.

Key Capabilities
  • "Help me write" Bubble: Trigger with Cmd/Ctrl + \ anywhere in the document.
  • Smart Chips & Canvas: Embed dropdowns, task lists, and @-mentions automatically.
  • Full Document Refactor: Change point of view, reformat as executive summary.
Featured Prompt:
Write an executive summary of this 40-page strategy report. Organize into: 1. Core Thesis, 2. Financial Trajectory, 3. Critical Risks, and 4. Immediate 30-60-90 Day Actions.

Google Slides

Imagen 3 & Presentation Outlines
Visual

Generate custom visuals on demand using Imagen 3 directly inside Slides, build full presentation decks, and create speaker notes.

Key Capabilities
  • Imagen 3 Text-to-Image: Create bespoke hero illustrations with artistic style controls.
  • Speaker Notes Generator: Synthesizes talking points for each slide.
  • Deck Structuring: Builds logical progression from problem to solution.
Featured Prompt:
A high-tech cloud network visualization showing interconnected fiber cables with glowing nodes, photorealistic, cinematic volumetric lighting, 8k resolution, minimalist dark aesthetic.

Google Meet

Take Notes For Me & Studio
Meetings

Focus entirely on conversation. Gemini logs notes in a Google Doc, captures action items, and enhances audio and visual quality.

Key Capabilities
  • "Take notes for me": Automatically documents decisions & creates action item table.
  • Translated Captions: Real-time translation across 60+ language pairs.
  • Studio Look & Sound: AI portrait lighting correction and audio noise isolation.
Pro Tip:

Enable "Take notes for me" in calendar invites to automatically deliver the summary doc to all attendees post-call.

Google Drive

Semantic Multi-Doc Q&A
Knowledge

Ask Gemini questions across multiple files simultaneously without opening them individually.

Key Capabilities
  • Cross-file Synthesis: Compare terms across 5 different customer agreements.
  • Concept Search: Find relevant files even if exact keywords aren't present.
Featured Prompt:
Find all agreements signed in 2025 containing an auto-renewal clause and generate a comparison table of termination notice periods.

Enterprise Privacy & Data Security Guarantee

How Google Workspace protects your organization's confidential data

1. No Model Training

Your company data, emails, prompts, generated content, and documents are never used to train Google's underlying Gemini models without your explicit consent.

2. Complete Tenant Isolation

Data remains strictly within your organization's existing Google Workspace tenant perimeter, respecting all existing access controls and DLP policies.

3. Compliance Certified

Complies with strict enterprise governance frameworks including SOC 1/2/3, ISO/IEC 27001, HIPAA compliance support, and EU GDPR requirements.
